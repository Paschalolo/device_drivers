A single combined word in Turkish might require a whole sentence in English. The high number comes from counting every theoretical math-like combination of its 3-letter root system, rather than actual words used in daily life. It kept its original Germanic roots, absorbed French after invasions, took Latin for science, and continues to borrow terms globally.
